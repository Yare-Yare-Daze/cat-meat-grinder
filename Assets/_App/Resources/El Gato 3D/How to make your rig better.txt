(for blender users importing "El Gato (with rig)") your rig might have extra bones on the end of El Gato's limbs

to solve that issue do the following actions:

1) Select the Skeleton (Armature) (while in object mode)
2) click the button on the top left corner labeled "Object mode"
3) choose "Edit mode"
4) select the excess bones
5) [delete] -> select "bones" NOT "dissovle bones"
6) Press "Edit mode" and switch to "Pose Mode" to start animating
